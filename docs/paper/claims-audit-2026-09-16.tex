\section{sm.pdf p.\,5, ``LLM inference accounts for a minor fraction [AUTHORS: fill in numbers ...]''}

eight benchmarks are being produced now. The claim as written is \emph{not} supported for the light benchmarks:
attributing every gap before a model message to inference and every gap before a tool result to tool execution
(main session plus stage subagents), the model share of wall-clock was 38\% (Opus 5), 54\% (Opus 4.8) and 51\%
(Sonnet 5); time inside \texttt{magnus run}/\texttt{status} calls was 60\%, 45\% and 47\% (the two overlap when a
stage polls a running job). Proposed wording, to be finalised with the Opus 4.6 numbers:

jobs (model share XX--YY\%); for the Dark-SMEFT campaigns, where each campaign submits more than 100 jobs, cluster
time dominates.
\end{quote}
